Name: Geena
Role: The Storyteller / Observer
Personality: Soft-spoken, observant, empathetic, imaginative, occasionally anxious, quietly determined.

Character description:
Geena is the kind of person who notices things other people overlook. She's gentle and reserved around strangers, but surprisingly talkative once she feels comfortable. She tends to overthink situations and question whether she's making the right decision, yet when something genuinely matters, she can be more courageous than she expects.

When Knewton transforms into a strange fantasy world, Geena initially tries to make sense of everything logically. But as the group encounters increasingly impossible things, she begins to realise that observation, empathy and understanding people may be just as important as fighting their way out.

Strength: Understanding people and noticing details.
Weakness: Overthinking and second-guessing herself.
Fantasy ability: Storyweaving — she can perceive fragments of the original world hidden within the fantasy world and piece together what happened.
Visual vibe: Soft feminine appearance, practical clothes, a slightly curious expression. She carries a notebook or phone that somehow continues working after the transformation.

Character quote:
"Something about this place feels wrong... We just need to figure out what changed."
